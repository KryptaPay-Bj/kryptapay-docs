---
title: "Deposits: bank, stablecoin and GIMAC"
description: "Fund your wallets by bank transfer, stablecoin or GIMAC. KryptaPay reviews each deposit before crediting it."
---

A **deposit** funds one of your wallets with money sent to KryptaPay outside the API: a **bank transfer**, a **stablecoin** transfer (USDC, USDT) or a **GIMAC** payment in the CEMAC zone. You declare it, and KryptaPay credits your wallet **after checking that the money arrived**.

<Info>
  Collecting from a customer's Mobile Money wallet is a [payin](/payins), not a deposit. Payins only accept Mobile Money networks: a bank or stablecoin network there returns `422`.
</Info>

Declaring deposits needs a key with the `payins:write` [scope](/authentication#scopes).

## Bank transfer

### 1. Get the bank details

```bash
curl "https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/collect-accounts?country=BJ" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY"
```

Each account comes with its currency, IBAN or account number, BIC/SWIFT and `notes` to follow for the transfer. An empty `items` list means no KryptaPay account is open in that country yet: contact support. See [List KryptaPay bank accounts](/api-reference/deposits/bank-accounts).

<Warning>
  Always take the bank details from this endpoint, never from an example or an old email.
</Warning>

### 2. Send the transfer, then declare it

Once the transfer is sent, declare it with the receipt as `proof` (PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP, base64-encoded):

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "channel": "BANK",
    "currency": "XOF",
    "declaredAmount": "2500000",
    "bankName": "Ecobank Bénin",
    "payerName": "Awa Koné SARL",
    "proof": { "filename": "wire-receipt.pdf", "mimeType": "application/pdf", "base64": "JVBERi0xLjcKJc..." }
  }'
```

The response holds the deposit `id`, a `reference` (`dep_…`) and a `ticketRef` (`DEP-…`) to quote to support. The whole request must stay under 15 MB.

Forgot the receipt, or need to replace it? [Attach a proof](/api-reference/deposits/attach-proof) while the deposit is still `PENDING`.

## Stablecoin

### 1. Get the deposit address

```bash
curl "https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits/stablecoin-address?asset=USDT&network=TRON" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY"
```

`asset` is `USDC` or `USDT`; `network` is `ETH`, `POLYGON` or `TRON`. `404 no_address` means no address is open for that pair.

<Warning>
  Send **only** the requested asset, **only** on the requested network. Funds sent with another asset or on another network may be lost. Always read the address from this endpoint.
</Warning>

### 2. Send on-chain, then declare it

A stablecoin deposit needs no file: the transaction hash is the proof.

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "channel": "STABLECOIN",
    "currency": "USDT",
    "declaredAmount": "1500",
    "network": "TRON",
    "txHash": "9f2c1e7a4b3d8f6e5a2c1b0d9e8f7a6b5c4d3e2f1a0b9c8d7e6f5a4b3c2d1e0f"
  }'
```

`currency` is the asset you sent: the deposit credits your `USDC` or `USDT` wallet.

## GIMAC

GIMAC lets a payer pay from a bank or mobile money account connected to GIMAC in the CEMAC zone (Cameroon, Congo, Gabon, Chad, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea), by scanning the KryptaPay QR code or entering its merchant code. The payer can be you, or one of your customers. GIMAC deposits are in `XAF` only.

Unlike a bank or stablecoin deposit, you declare a GIMAC deposit **before** the payment.

### 1. Check that GIMAC is available

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits/gimac \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY"
```

Offer GIMAC only when `available` is `true`. The response also gives the `merchantName` and `merchantCode`, whether a QR code exists (`hasQr`), and the entry fee of each country in `fees` (basis points: `200` = 2 %). See [Get the GIMAC payment details](/api-reference/deposits/gimac).

### 2. Declare the deposit

Ask the payer for the country of the account they pay from: it sets the entry fee.

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "channel": "GIMAC",
    "currency": "XAF",
    "declaredAmount": "50000",
    "payerCountry": "CM",
    "payerName": "Paul Mbarga"
  }'
```

The deposit is `PENDING`, with a `ticketRef` (`DEP-…`), the fee rate frozen in `feeBps`, and an indicative deadline in `expiresAt` (30 minutes). No proof is needed.

### 3. Show the payer what to pay

Show the payer, together:

- the QR code, from [Get the GIMAC QR code](/api-reference/deposits/gimac-qr) (an image), or the `merchantCode` when there is no QR code;
- the **exact** amount (`declaredAmount`);
- the `ticketRef`, to put in the **payment description**.

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/deposits/gimac/qr \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY" \
  --output gimac-qr.png
```

Fetch the image from your server: your API key must never reach the payer's browser.

<Warning>
  Without the `ticketRef` in the payment description, KryptaPay cannot match the payment to the deposit quickly.
</Warning>

### 4. Get the result

KryptaPay matches the payment on the GIMAC statement, then approves the deposit. The entry fee of the payer's country is deducted: `confirmedAmount` is the amount received, `feeAmount` the fee, and `creditedAmount` what reaches your wallet. A payment that arrives after `expiresAt` can still be approved.

## Review and crediting

Every deposit is **reviewed by KryptaPay** before your wallet is credited:

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | Declared, waiting for KryptaPay to confirm the money arrived. |
| `APPROVED` | Confirmed: your wallet is credited with `confirmedAmount`, the amount actually received (for `GIMAC`, `creditedAmount`, after the entry fee). It also appears in your [transactions](/api-reference/transactions/list). |
| `REJECTED` | Not credited. `decisionNote` explains why. |

When KryptaPay has reviewed a deposit, you receive the `DEPOSIT_APPROVED` or `DEPOSIT_REJECTED` [webhook](/webhooks#supported-events): subscribe your endpoint to them. You can also poll [Get a deposit](/api-reference/deposits/get) with its `id`, `reference` or `ticketRef`, or [list your deposits](/api-reference/deposits/list).

`confirmedAmount` can be lower than `declaredAmount` when banks or the network took fees on the way.

## In sandbox

The sandbox accepts deposit declarations so you can test your integration, but no money moves: a sandbox deposit stays `PENDING` unless you ask support to approve or reject it, to test how you handle the result and the webhooks. Sandbox deposits notify your sandbox webhook endpoints only. A sandbox key is refused on the production API (`403 env_mismatch`).

## Common errors

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `validation_error` | 422 | A required field is missing or wrong, e.g. `proof` for a bank transfer, `txHash` for a stablecoin transfer, `payerCountry` or a currency other than `XAF` for GIMAC. |
| `gimac_unavailable` | 409 | GIMAC deposits are temporarily unavailable. Check `available` in [Get the GIMAC payment details](/api-reference/deposits/gimac). |
| `too_large`, `mime_not_allowed` | 422 | The proof file is too big or not an accepted type. |
| `bad_status` | 409 | You tried to attach a proof to a deposit KryptaPay has already reviewed. |
| `no_address` | 404 | No stablecoin address is open for that asset and network. |
| `not_found` (QR code) | 404 | No GIMAC QR code is set: show the `merchantCode` instead. |
| `not_found` | 404 | No deposit with that id, `reference` or `ticketRef` on your account. |
