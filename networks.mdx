---
title: "Networks and currencies"
description: "Payment networks, countries and currencies you can use with KryptaPay."
---

Every payin and payout names a `network`: a Mobile Money operator, a bank rail or a blockchain. The `currency` must match the network.

<Tip>
  Operators occasionally go into maintenance. Call [List available networks](/api-reference/networks/list) to get the networks usable right now, and hide the others from your customers.
</Tip>

## Stablecoins

| Network code | Chain | Token standard | Currencies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ETH` | Ethereum | ERC-20 | USDT, USDC |
| `TRON` | Tron | TRC-20 | USDT, USDC |
| `POLYGON` | Polygon PoS | ERC-20 | USDT, USDC |

<Warning>
  Always send stablecoins on the chain you selected. Funds sent on the wrong chain can be lost.
</Warning>
