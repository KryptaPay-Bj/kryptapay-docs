---
title: "Beneficiaries"
description: "Save who you pay, and get EUR / USD beneficiaries verified before paying them."
---

A **beneficiary** is a person or company you pay, saved with one or more payment accounts. Bank payouts in `EUR` or `USD` must target a saved beneficiary. For other networks it is optional.

Managing beneficiaries needs a key with the `payouts:write` [scope](/authentication#scopes).

## Individual or company

Choose `entityType` when you create the beneficiary. You can't change it afterwards.

| `entityType` | Accounts allowed | Documents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BUSINESS` (default) | Bank, Mobile Money and stablecoin, in any supported currency | Trade register extract (`rccm`) and the manager's ID (`id_director`) are required |
| `INDIVIDUAL` | `EUR` and `USD` bank accounts only | None. Their identity is verified by our payment partner |

## 1. Create the beneficiary

```bash
curl https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/counterparties \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "entityType": "INDIVIDUAL",
    "fullName": "Jean Dupont",
    "country": "FR",
    "email": "jean.dupont@example.com",
    "accounts": [
      {
        "kind": "BANK",
        "currency": "EUR",
        "bankName": "BNP Paribas",
        "accountHolder": "Jean Dupont",
        "iban": "FR7630006000011234567890189",
        "swiftBic": "BNPAFRPP"
      }
    ]
  }'
```

- `accounts` holds at most one account per `kind` and `currency`.
- `email` is required as soon as the beneficiary has an `EUR` or `USD` bank account: the verification link is sent there.
- A company sends its documents in `documents`, each as `{ kind, filename, mimeType, base64 }`. The request body can be up to 50 MB.

The response is the saved beneficiary. Keep its `id`: you pass it as `counterpartyId` when you create a payout.

## 2. Approval and verification

A new beneficiary starts with `approvalStatus: "PENDING"`. You can pay it once it is `APPROVED`.

If the beneficiary has an `EUR` or `USD` bank account, we email them a link from our payment partner. They use it to:

1. Verify their identity (individual) or their company and its beneficial owners (company).
2. Accept our partner's terms of service.

Their `EUR` and `USD` accounts can be paid only once both steps are done. Their other accounts follow the usual approval.

The beneficiary's `verification` object tells you where they are:

```json
{
  "verification": {
    "status": "UNDER_REVIEW",
    "tosAccepted": false,
    "nameMismatch": false,
    "lastSentAt": "2026-11-11T10:15:00.000Z",
    "rejectionReasons": []
  }
}
```

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `NOT_STARTED` | The beneficiary hasn't opened the link yet. |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | They started but haven't finished. |
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | Our partner is reviewing what they sent. |
| `ACTION_REQUIRED` | Our partner needs more information from them. |
| `APPROVED` | Verified. Payable once `tosAccepted` is also `true`. |
| `REJECTED` | Verification refused. `rejectionReasons` says why. Their `EUR` / `USD` accounts can't be paid. |
| `SUSPENDED` | Our partner suspended the beneficiary. Their `EUR` / `USD` accounts can't be paid. |

`nameMismatch: true` means the same email was already verified under another name. KryptaPay checks it is the same person or company before the verification applies.

<Note>
  A beneficiary saved before verification existed has `verification: null`. Verification doesn't block their payouts until you send them the link with the call below.
</Note>

## 3. Resend the link

If the beneficiary lost the email, send the link again:

```bash
curl -X POST https://staging.api.krypta-pay.com/v1/counterparties/cmh1a2b3c0001xyz/verification \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KRYPTAPAY_API_KEY"
```

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `no_verified_account` | 422 | The beneficiary has no `EUR` or `USD` bank account. |
| `email_required` | 422 | Add the beneficiary's email first. |
| `country_not_supported` | 422 | Our partner can't verify beneficiaries living in this country for this currency. |
| `already_verified` | 409 | Nothing to send: the beneficiary is verified. |
| `resend_too_soon` | 409 | The link was sent less than 10 minutes ago. |
| `verification_unavailable` | 409 | Verification is temporarily unavailable. Retry later. |
| `verification_provider_error` | 502 | Our partner didn't answer. Retry later. |

## Editing a beneficiary

`PATCH /v1/counterparties/{id}` updates a beneficiary. Any change sends it back to `PENDING` until KryptaPay reviews it again. Changing its email starts a new verification at the new address.

## Paying a beneficiary

Pass the beneficiary's `id` as `counterpartyId`, and the account details you saved for it:

```json
{
  "amount": "1500",
  "currency": "EUR",
  "network": "BANK_EUR",
  "counterpartyId": "cmh1a2b3c0001xyz",
  "recipient": { "iban": "FR7630006000011234567890189", "fullName": "Jean Dupont" }
}
```

For `BANK_EUR` and `BANK_USD`, `recipient.iban` (or `recipient.accountNumber`) must be the beneficiary's saved account in that currency. Spaces and letter case don't matter. See [Send payouts](/payouts#bank-payouts-in-eur-and-usd) for the errors.

In the sandbox, a beneficiary doesn't need approval or verification to be paid. `counterpartyId` and the matching account are still required for `EUR` and `USD`, so your integration behaves the same in production.
