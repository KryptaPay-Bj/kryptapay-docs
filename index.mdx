## How it fits together

- **Wallets** hold your balance, one per currency. Payins credit them, payouts and fees debit them.
- **Payins** collect money from a customer into a wallet.
- **Payouts** send money from a wallet to a recipient.
- **Conversions** move value between two of your wallets in different currencies.
- **Transactions** are the unified history of all of the above.

Every payin, payout and conversion is asynchronous: the API answers immediately with `PENDING` or `PROCESSING`, and the final result arrives by [webhook](/webhooks).
